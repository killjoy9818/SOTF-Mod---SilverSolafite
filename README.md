# SilverSolafite

Tired of the standard gold look—or want a little extra power from your upgrades? SilverSolafite overhauls the appearance of Solafite across Sons of the Forest, swapping out the default gold textures and materials for a sleek, metallic silver finish right out of the box, while also letting you customize Solafite buff strength.
Whether you want your plated armor, upgraded weapons, or Solafite structures to match a colder aesthetic, want a completely custom color, or want to tune how strong Solafite enhancements are, this mod gives you full control.

Features

• Instant Silver Conversion: Automatically replaces default gold Solafite with a clean silver finish upon installation.
• 8 Built-in Color Presets: Easily switch between curated metallic and gemstone finishes:
• Silver (Default)
• Original Gold
• Platinum
• Cobalt Blue
• Obsidian
• Rose Gold
• Emerald
• Crimson
• Full RGB Color Selector: Dial in any custom shade or hue using the built-in RGB sliders to create your own unique look.
• Customizable Buff Multiplier: Adjust the effectiveness of Solafite buffs and upgrades to match your preferred playstyle—keep it vanilla (1.0x), boost your power, or tone it down.

Installation

Ensure you have RedLoader installed for Sons of the Forest.
Download the SilverSolafite release archive.
Extract the .dll file and Folder into your game's Mods folder.
Launch the game and open the mod configuration menu to switch presets or customize your RGB values.
Compatibility

• Works with existing saves—no new game required.
• Purely visual/client-side; safe to use in multiplayer sessions (other players without the mod will still see standard gold Solafite).
