SilverSolafite#
Tired of the standard gold look—or want more control over your upgrade visuals and power? SilverSolafite overhauls Solafite plating across Sons of the Forest, swapping default gold for a sleek metallic silver finish right out of the box while giving you independent color presets, surface sheen controls, live hotkeys with on-screen HUD feedback, and a customizable power multiplier.

Whether you want your weapons, armor, base walls, and Solafite ore to each have their own distinct metallic or gemstone finish—or want to tune how strong your plated gear and structures are—this mod gives you full control.

✨ Key Features#
Instant Silver Conversion: Automatically replaces default gold Solafite plating with a clean silver finish upon installation (plus a matching silver finish on upgrader bones and skulls).
11 Independent Category Presets: Mix and match colors across your entire arsenal and base instead of using one global color. Assign separate presets to:
Weapons & Tools
Armor
Log Walls
Defense Walls & Gates
Stone Walls
Floors & Roofs
Stairs & Ramps
Doors
Furniture & Racks
Other Plated Items (Upgraders, Shrines, Artifacts, Teleporters)
Solafite Ore
8 Built-in Color Presets + Custom RGB: Choose from 8 curated finishes or dial in your own color via RGB sliders:
0 — Silver (Default)
1 — Original Gold (Vanilla)
2 — Metallic Violet
3 — Cobalt Blue
4 — Obsidian
5 — Rose Gold
6 — Emerald
7 — Crimson
8 — Custom RGB (Plus a global toggle to override all categories with your Custom RGB at once)
Surface Sheen & Crystal Glow Sliders: Fine-tune Metallic Intensity (0.00–1.00), Smoothness / Gloss Sheen (0.00–1.00), and Crystal Crack Glow (0.00–3.00) to make your plating matte, mirror-polished, or intensely glowing.
Live In-Game Hotkeys with HUD Feedback: Cycle through categories and swap color presets on the fly with a color-coded on-screen notification showing the active category and preset.
Customizable Power Multiplier (1x to 50x): Scale melee and ranged weapon damage, player armor damage reduction, and plated structure toughness (defaults to 5.0x; set to 1.0x for vanilla stats).
🎮 Controls & Configuration#
You can adjust all settings in-game through the RedLoader Settings UI or by editing UserData\SilverSolafite.cfg:

Cycle Target Category: Numpad - (NumpadMinus) — Cycles through the 11 plating categories and displays the selected category on screen.
Cycle Color Preset: Numpad + (NumpadPlus) — Cycles through presets 0–8 for the currently selected category with a color-matched HUD popup.
Re-Scan Placed Structures Now: Toggle this ON in the settings menu after building or plating new base structures to immediately apply category colors.
(Note: Hotkeys can be rebound to any Unity Key name or set to None to disable in the config.)

📥 Installation#
Ensure you have RedLoader installed for Sons of the Forest.
Download the latest SilverSolafite release archive.
Extract the SilverSolafite.zip Mods folder into your game installation folder.
Launch the game and open the RedLoader mod settings menu to customize your presets, sheen, hotkeys, and power multiplier.
🌐 Multiplayer & Compatibility#
Save Compatibility: Works seamlessly with existing saves—no new game required.
Multiplayer: Visual changes are client-side and safe to use in multiplayer sessions (other players without the mod will see standard gold Solafite). Note that the Plating Power Multiplier modifies local weapon damage, incoming player damage, and structure durability—set it to 1.0x if you want strictly vanilla balance in co-op.
Used for HOTKEYS#
Default is NumpadMinus and NumpadPlus.

Letters. A through Z
Top Row Numbers. Digit0 through Digit9
Numpad. Numpad0 through Numpad9, NumpadEnter, NumpadPlus, NumpadMinus, NumpadMultiply, NumpadDivide, NumpadPeriod.
Arrow Keys. UpArrow, DownArrow, LeftArrow, RightArrow.
Modifiers. LeftShift, RightShift, LeftCtrl, RightCtrl, LeftAlt, RightAlt, LeftMeta (Windows/Command), RightMeta.
Special & Navigation. Space, Enter, Escape, Tab, Backspace, Delete, Insert, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown, CapsLock.
Function Keys. F1 through F12.
Punctuation. Comma, Period, Slash, Backslash, Semicolon, Quote, LeftBracket, RightBracket, Minus, Equals, Backquote
